What We Offer

Infant Care (0-18m)

Nurturing, sensory-focused care for our youngest learners. High staff-to-child ratio.

Toddler Program (18m-3y)

Focus on language, motor skills, and social play in a safe environment.

Preschool (3-5y)

School readiness, early literacy, numeracy, and creative expression.

Inclusive Support

On-site therapists, sensory rooms, and individualized education plans for special needs.

Nutrition Program

Chef-prepared, balanced meals catering to all dietary restrictions and allergies.

Aftercare & Holiday Club

Safe transport and supervised homework assistance for school-going siblings.
