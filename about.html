Our Story & Vision

Meet the Founder

Founded by Precious Tebogo Mokgobu in 2026, LittleBlossom was born out of a simple realization: parents of children with unique abilities and needs were struggling to find a daycare that would not just accept their child, but truly celebrate them.

We set out to build a center that breaks the mold. A place where a child with autism, a shy child, a highly energetic child, and a child with physical disabilities could all learn, play, and grow side-by-side.
